# Master Quality Rubric (100 points)
| Dimension | Points | What to inspect |
|---|---:|---|
| Story and emotional impact | 15 | Causality, stakes, payoff, theme, emotional clarity |
| Screenplay and dialogue | 10 | Structure, scene turns, pacing, voice distinction |
| Character and continuity | 10 | Motivation, arc, appearance/wardrobe/props continuity |
| Direction and performance | 10 | Blocking, intention, performance coherence |
| Visual and cinematography | 15 | Composition, lighting, color, focus, visual storytelling |
| Motion and generation stability | 10 | Temporal coherence, artifacts, motion plausibility |
| Audio and music | 10 | Dialogue clarity, sync, mix, rights |
| Editing and rhythm | 10 | Continuity, pace, transitions, narrative clarity |
| Technical delivery | 5 | Resolution, frame rate, codec, subtitles, file integrity |
| Originality, culture and rights | 5 | Originality, cultural accuracy, license/provenance |

## Thresholds
- 95–100 MASTER
- 90–94 PREMIUM PASS
- 80–89 REVISION REQUIRED
- 60–79 MAJOR REVISION
- 0–59 FAIL

## Critical failures (automatic FAIL/BLOCK)
- Missing/unclear rights for material intended for commercial use
- Broken or corrupt final file
- Major mismatch with user's hard constraints
- Unintelligible dialogue or severe audio/video sync failure
- Material continuity contradiction that breaks story comprehension
- Fabricated Top-10 evidence or unsupported ranking claims
- Unapproved real-person likeness/voice use where consent/rights are required

Every score must cite evidence: file, scene/shot, timestamp, test result or reviewer note.
