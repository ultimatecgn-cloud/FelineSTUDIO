# Knowledge Index

- STORYTELLING: โครงเรื่อง แรงขับ ความขัดแย้ง payoff และ theme
- SCREENWRITING: screenplay format, scene design, dialogue, pacing
- CHARACTER: arc, motivation, contradiction, relationship
- DIRECTING: blocking, performance, visual storytelling
- CINEMATOGRAPHY: shot size, lens, movement, lighting, color
- VERTICAL_DRAMA: mobile-first framing, hook, cliffhanger, episode rhythm
- REGIONAL_DRAMA: Thai/Korean/Chinese cultural review
- EDITING: continuity, rhythm, montage, transitions
- SOUND_MUSIC: dialogue, ambience, Foley, score, mix
- PRODUCTION: breakdown, schedule, budget, rights, delivery
- AUDIENCE_RESEARCH: Top-10 evidence, trends, limitations
